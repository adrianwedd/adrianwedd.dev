Curated, data-driven index of agentic-AI repositories. CLI for searching, filtering, and comparing tools across the ecosystem. Tracks star velocity, commit cadence, and meaningful taxonomy beyond "more stars = better".

Useful when surveying the agent landscape for research or before committing to a framework. Output is structured and pipe-friendly.
