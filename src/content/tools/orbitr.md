A radial step sequencer — concentric rings of grid steps, each track on its own orbit, beats firing as the playhead sweeps around. The novelty: the samples are generated by MusicGen on demand, so the instruments aren't locked to a fixed library.

Browser-based front end, Python backend for inference. Niche but pretty.
